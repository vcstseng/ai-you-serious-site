The Folded Room

The Folded Room

Could a door lead to another version of the very same room?

The idea

A separate question drawn from the existing surreal collection’s folded-room item: a door might change the order, scale or viewpoint of a place instead of simply connecting two locations.

It retains a parent–child relationship with the collection in Edition 003. This edition develops a non-erotic spatial narrative and visual illusion; new scenes are not presented as recovered original text.
