Digital Antiquities Exchange

Who might still want to keep an obsolete digital object?

The idea

Treat obsolete digital objects as a collecting topic involving rereading, preservation and circulation.

Editorial status
Original collection item; organized as a concept for this edition

Original notes

Digital Antiquities Exchange is an original item in the finance and exchange section of Master Archive #218. The available source is a title; it does not define eligible objects or how possession and transfer rights would be established.

This edition therefore leaves collecting, circulation and preservation open. It does not invent an NFT, token or investment model for the original idea, and no exchange has been built.
