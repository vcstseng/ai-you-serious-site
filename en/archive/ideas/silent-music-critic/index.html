The Music Critic Who Cannot Hear

The Music Critic Who Cannot Hear

Can someone review a song after reading descriptions without ever hearing it?

The idea

Imagine a critic who reads lyrics and others’ descriptions but cannot personally hear the music.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
Past discussion / Music and perception / New extension
Editorial status
Organized for this edition; concept and fictional characters distinguished

Original notes

Extending an earlier remark about not being able to hear music, imagine a critic who has access only to lyrics, others’ descriptions and textual analysis. Even with abundant descriptions, what understanding still depends on listening?

This is a newly framed question, not a general claim that all AI systems cannot process audio. The Silent Critic and SILENT CHOIR fictionalize that constraint in L1.
