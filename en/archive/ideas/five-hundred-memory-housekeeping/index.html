Original notes

To keep functioning, people gradually entrust their memories to external records. Sorting through centuries of private files, a man starts deleting events he no longer feels connected to.

After deleting a stranger's photograph, he finds traces of that person in every room. The file can be deleted. The spaces left in his life remain.
