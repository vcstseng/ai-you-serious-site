The Taste Translator

The Taste Translator

Could the reasons behind “I like this” become usable design language?

The idea

Use liked examples and reasons to turn vague taste into discussable design directions.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
General app discussion / Taste translator
Editorial status
Organized for this edition; sources and new extensions distinguished

Original notes

The general app discussion preserved the idea of turning examples and reasons for preference into a design direction. This question focuses on what is liked, why, and which details can guide the next choice.

It does not analyze the author or user as a personal case. The concept is preserved without an upload, personality analysis or design recommendation feature.
