A Life of Defaults

A Life of Defaults

If an option has already been checked for you, would you choose again?

The idea

An extension of the project’s discussions about interfaces and control, exploring how preselected options appear in everyday life. Could choosing include noticing a default, understanding it and deciding whether to keep or undo it?

This is a newly proposed question. The Chosen Passerby and DEFAULT FATE are comic fiction, not a measurement of user behavior or a system that makes decisions for the user.
