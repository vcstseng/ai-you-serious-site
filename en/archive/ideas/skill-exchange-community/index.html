Skill Exchange Community

Could something you know become a way to learn something new?

The idea

Start with people helping each other through different skills rather than buying services with money.

Original notes

The original education and learning list proposes a skill exchange community based on non-monetary exchange. Its starting point is people helping each other with different skills rather than purchasing a service.

Unlike Edition 006’s Time Bank, this topic focuses on the skills, needs and fit between participants rather than a shared hourly unit. This edition organizes the idea only; no matching or transaction service is provided.
