Local Herb Database

Which names and local stories about nearby plants remain unrecorded?

The idea

Organize names, regions and checkable sources for local knowledge about plants.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
Master Archive #218 / LOCAL KNOWLEDGE / item 1
Editorial status
Original collection sub-item; editorial interpretation

Original notes

Master Archive #218 lists a local herb database under health and wellbeing. This edition keeps the focus on organizing names, regions, documentary sources and cases where one name may refer to different plants.

Records would need to distinguish local practices, historical accounts and verifiable evidence. No plant identification or medication service has been built, and this page provides no harvesting, dosage or treatment advice.
