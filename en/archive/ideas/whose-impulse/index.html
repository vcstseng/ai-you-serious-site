Whose Impulse Is It, Anyway?

Whose Impulse Is It, Anyway?

When AI takes over execution, does human impulse disappear—or hide inside the rules?

The idea

An extension of the crypto-trading and agent discussions, focusing on the people who set rules, adjust goals, pause a system and take over execution. Delegating actions may move human choices to a different place.

This is a newly proposed question about control and behavior, not a recovered strategy specification. No trading agent, order execution or account connection is provided.
