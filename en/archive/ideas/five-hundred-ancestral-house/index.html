The Ancestral House

There hasn't been an empty room in our house for three hundred years.

The idea

If the previous generation never leaves, where can the next generation make room for itself?

Just be patient a little longer. One day, this will all be yours.

Original notes

Whenever another generation starts a family, another floor is added to the ancestral house. Nine generations share the building. The original owner still lives on the ground floor and refuses to sell, divide or move.

The cutaway opens floor by floor. The stairs narrow. Pipes pass through everyone's bedrooms. The children on the top floor have never been down to the ground.

Just be patient a little longer. One day, this will all be yours.
