A Person Becomes a Book

A Person Becomes a Book

If a book absorbed a person, which page would they become?

The idea

Imagine a person as a book to explore identity through characters, marginalia, blanks and skipped pages.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
100 Exotic Ideas / Objects and settings / 9
Editorial status
Organized for this edition; sources and new extensions distinguished

Original notes

The item about a book absorbing a person becomes a fictional question about narrative identity: a character, a footnote, or a page someone skips?

It retains its source relationship with the collection in Edition 003, adapted here into a non-erotic paper story. Specific events are still to be written and will not be presented as recovered original material.
