Traditional Crafts in AR

Could seeing a finished object also reveal how it was made?

The idea

Bring digital viewing closer to the materials, steps and practitioners behind a craft.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
Master Archive #218 / CRAFT & CULTURE / item 5
Editorial status
Original collection item; organized as a concept for this edition

Original notes

Traditional crafts in AR appears under community and culture in Master Archive #218. It connects craft with augmented reality, but the recovered source names no specific craft or completed exhibition.

Steps, materials and explanations from practitioners could be explored with clear attribution. No camera or AR feature is implemented, and a digital presentation is not treated as a replacement for practical skill.
