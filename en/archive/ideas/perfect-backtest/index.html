A Perfect Life in Backtests

A Perfect Life in Backtests

If you could start over every time, would you be good at trading—or at explaining the past?

The idea

A new extension of the earlier crypto-trading discussion, contrasting what can be explained afterward with what could have been known at the time. It asks how retrospective narratives form and what success means when conditions can be repeatedly adjusted.

This is not recovered text from an earlier trading strategy. No dataset, performance calculation or backtest has been implemented; the page preserves the question.
