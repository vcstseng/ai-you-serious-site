A Brighter Head, a Brighter Future?

A Brighter Head, a Brighter Future?

What absurd social map would appear if hair were treated as a status signal?

The idea

Start with the absurd question of whether a shinier head means greater success to examine status signals.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
Past discussion / A brighter head
Editorial status
Organized for this edition; sources and new extensions distinguished

Original notes

The earlier question about a brighter head and greater success becomes an entry into satire: how do appearance, presence and memorable traits lead us to invent stories about strangers’ status and abilities?

The subject is how signals and bias are read. The premise is not a judgment of real people’s abilities or a verified relationship between hair and success.
