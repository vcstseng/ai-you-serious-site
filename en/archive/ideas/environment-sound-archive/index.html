The Environmental Sound Archive

The Environmental Sound Archive

Can a place’s memory be preserved through sound?

The idea

Starting from the environmental-sound archive item, imagine preserving a sense of place through sound rather than photographs. Time, location, the recorder and vanished sound sources may all shape how a recording is understood.

The subject is local soundscapes, distinct from Edition 003’s Human Archive of personal portraits. The Street Listener and ECHO ADDRESS are fictional representations; this page collects no recordings of real people.
