Original notes

The family gathers for an elder's five hundredth birthday portrait. The photographer keeps stepping back and asking everyone to move closer, but still cannot fit them all in.

As the camera pulls back, the faces become an indistinguishable field of dots. The elder at the center remains clear. The children at the edges become too small to see.

Good. This time, nobody's missing.
