Interactive Fiction

Where can a reader’s choice take a story?

The idea

Let readers’ choices take part in a story’s movement so reading becomes part of its telling.

Sources and original notes ＋

Original collection sub-item; editorial interpretation

Original notes

Master Archive #218 lists interactive fiction under digital content and the creator economy. This topic keeps the relationship between reading, choice and storytelling open, without prescribing a genre or business model.

A short passage and two choices could be enough to explore how a decision changes what a reader encounters. This edition preserves the concept; branching fiction and an L3 game have not been built.
