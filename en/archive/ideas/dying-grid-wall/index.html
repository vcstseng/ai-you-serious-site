The Dying Grid Wall

The Dying Grid Wall

What if the ad you bought could die, be taken over, or be distorted by AI?

The idea

An advertising wall with a lifecycle: cells decay, brands mutate and positions can be taken over.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
Master Archive #221
Editorial status
Recovered concept; not implemented

Original notes

An advertising wall becomes an experiment in uncontrollable exposure: spaces expire, brands mutate and positions can be taken over. The original concept includes anti-advertising weights, battles for spaces and a Coffin Grid.
