Late-Night Food Platform

It is late. Where nearby can you still sit down for a meal?

The idea

Connect people who need a late meal with places that are still serving.

Sources and original notes ＋

Original notes

The original food and dining list includes a late-night food platform. It concerns people looking for a meal outside conventional dining hours and the places that are still open.

Opening hours, dine-in versus takeaway and keeping information accurate would be useful starting points. This is a concept file, with no live venue information, reservations or delivery service.
