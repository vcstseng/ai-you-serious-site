Micro Robot Kits

What single action would make a tiny robot interesting?

The idea

Master Archive #218 lists micro robot kits under technology applications. The recovered concept leaves room for small robots that can be assembled and explored, without assuming an all-purpose assistant.

One action, one type of sensing or one simple rule could provide a starting point. This edition includes no hardware kit, wiring instructions or completed robot.
