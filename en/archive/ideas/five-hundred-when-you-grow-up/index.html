Original notes

From the age of twenty, a man is repeatedly told by his elders to wait. At forty, eighty and a hundred and sixty, someone in the family is still better qualified to decide his life.

He finally takes the head seat at the family meeting. His first words to the next generation are: 'You don't understand yet.'

He has finally become a mature person.
