Original notes

Every year, the staff prepare a cake, speeches and a succession plan. At the last moment, the chairman always announces that he will stay with everyone a little longer.

In every photograph on the wall, the chairman sits in the middle. One by one, the promising young successors become the old people sitting beside him.

Don't be impatient, young man. You're only a hundred and twenty.
