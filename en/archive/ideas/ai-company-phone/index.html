Does an AI Company Need a Phone?

Does an AI Company Need a Phone?

Must the people who build a model also control the doorway through which we use it?

The idea

Use the model-and-phone hypothesis to explore how a capability relates to the interface through which it is used.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
Past discussion / Apple and AI / Hypothetical device question
Editorial status
Organized for this edition; concept and fictional characters distinguished

Original notes

Drawn from the earlier hypothetical question about Claude or ChatGPT making their own phones, this idea explores the relationship between models, devices and access. A capability can be used through someone else’s interface, or designed together with an interface of its own.

This remains a thought experiment about product boundaries, not a report of any company’s current plans. The Gate Broker and POCKET KING are newly created fictional characters for this edition.
