How to Pretend Your Life Is Great

Is a beautiful life on social media a record or a performance?

The idea

Treat social images of a perfect life as a satirical topic between documentation and performance.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
HOW TO FAIL Titles / Recovered order 1 / Title only
Editorial status
Title recovered; framing and characters newly created

Original notes

A recovered title from the HOW TO FAIL series whose full text remains missing. This edition uses documentation versus performance as a curatorial question; that framing and the character story are new, not recovered arguments from the original.

The Life Curator and GOLDEN FEED turn framing, editing and what gets left out into a fictional power. This is not a judgment of real people or accounts, and no social-media data is collected.
