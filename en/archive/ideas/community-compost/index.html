The Community Compost Alliance

The Community Compost Alliance

If food scraps became the next round’s material, how would neighbors cooperate?

The idea

Starting from the community compost alliance item, this question focuses on cooperation within a resource cycle: who supplies materials, maintains the process, waits for the result and decides how it is shared?

This is a concept about local cooperation, not an operating service or practical recipe. The Neighborhood Gardener and SECOND BLOOM fictionalize cooperation and care.
