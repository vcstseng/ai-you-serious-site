The Museum of Unmade Buttons

The Museum of Unmade Buttons

What can a button that has not been invented promise through its name alone?

The idea

Collect buttons whose apparent functions exist only in imagination.

Sources and original notes ＋
Source record
Current interface discussion / New extension
Editorial status
Organized for this edition; concept and fictional characters distinguished

Original notes

Extending discussions about interface controls and expectations, imagine a museum of functions that do not exist. How do labels, shapes and placement lead us to infer what a button should do?

This is an explicitly imagined exhibition concept. The Mechanism Collector and FALSE PROMISE are fictional adaptations; nonexistent functions are not presented as working website controls.
