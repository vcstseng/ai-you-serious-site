Original notes

Two families stop speaking over a minor slight. Three hundred years later, those involved are still alive, and their descendants are required to obey the ban.

The original parties can no longer remember the details of the quarrel. Each generation, however, adds new reasons to keep the hatred alive.

If I forgive him now, what were those three hundred years of hatred for?
