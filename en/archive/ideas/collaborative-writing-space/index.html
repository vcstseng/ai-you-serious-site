Original notes

The original work and careers list proposes a collaborative writing space. The title concerns an environment for writing together; it does not require AI or prescribe a particular content format.

Shared drafts, suggested changes, attribution and versions could provide practical starting points. This edition organizes the concept only, without real-time collaborative editing or document storage.
