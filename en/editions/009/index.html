The fictional world of this edition

If humans could live for five hundred years while family, work, property, contracts and power stayed the same, what would life become?

  • Humans can live to about five hundred, but disease and accidents can still kill them.
  • People reach adulthood at roughly the same pace as today, then age much more slowly. Bodies do not reset; time, injuries and illness still leave marks.
  • Memory capacity, wealth distribution, succession and relationships have not been solved alongside longevity.
  • Many generations living at once is normal. The horror comes from ordinary institutions continuing for centuries.

This is social-horror fiction, not an introduction to longevity technology.

Five hundred years is a long time.
But the places in the family stay the same.
